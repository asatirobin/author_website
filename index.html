The Distance to Yesterday

By Robin Asati
🏆 Winner of the 21st Century Emily Dickinson Award

The Distance to Yesterday is a journey through memory, love, and time. In these poems, childhood games return with the scent of rain-soaked paper boats, young love lingers in unspoken silences, and the quiet strength of family echoes through pious stories told at dusk. Balancing nostalgia with reflection, this collection moves from the carefree innocence of youth to the unspoken struggles of adulthood.

About

I’m a writer and lifelong reader fascinated by ideas and the ways they shape the world. My work draws on a wide range of interests, including history, creativity, and learning, but I’m less interested in disciplines themselves than in the questions that connect them. Reading widely and thinking deeply are central to my approach, and much of my writing begins with a desire to understand something more clearly.

This website is a home for my books, general musings, notes, and ongoing projects. It serves as a record of ideas explored, questions pursued, and lessons gathered through years of reading, writing, and study.
